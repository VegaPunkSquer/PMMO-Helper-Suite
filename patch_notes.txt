Patch Notes v0.1 - v2.0.8 - The Silph Scope
Here are the technical details for curious Trainers:

Hello, Trainers!

Welcome to the definitive version of The Silph Scope! My name is Vega, and this project is my passion for Pokémon and the 3D Pokémon MMO turned into a complete toolkit.

To celebrate this release, I’ve prepared a summary of the app's "evolution," detailing the technical journey that brought us here.

v0.1 - The Foundation
NEW

Collection Manager: The core of the app. A system to add, edit, and save Pokémon with essential data (level, nature, IVs, EVs, notes).

v0.3 - The Dawn of Strategy
NEW

Team Analyzer (Basic): An initial tool to build a team of up to 6 Pokémon and generate a detailed analysis of their defensive weaknesses and resistances.

v0.5 - Simulating Combat
NEW

Battle Planner (Beta): First implementation of a boss battle simulation, calculating basic counters purely based on type advantage.

v0.8 - Master’s Tools
NEW

EV Training Guide: An integrated database suggesting the best locations and Pokémon for training specific EVs, including location maps.

NEW

Shiny Hunt Tracker: A module with a timer and encounter counter to help organize and keep records of your hunts.

v1.0 - The Great Logic Overhaul
UPDATE

Battle Planner 2.0: The system left Beta. The simulation logic was rebuilt from scratch to include a deeper analysis of base stats, damage, and survival time, resulting in a much more accurate "Battle Index."

UPDATE

Database: Added move data, new Pokémon, and bosses to power the new battle logic.

v1.1 - The Machine's Eye
NEW

Stats Calculator (OCR): Implementation of an Optical Character Recognition system. By pressing F10, the app captures a screen region and automatically extracts stats to quickly add them to the collection.

v1.2 - Refinement & Quality of Life
NEW

Themes and Languages: Added support for multiple themes (Dark, Light, Pokémon) and languages (PT-BR, EN-US), selectable in the new Settings tab.

NEW

Global Sorting: Implemented an option in Settings to define the default sort order (Alphabetical or Pokédex), which is now respected across all Pokémon lists in the app.

FIX

Various fixes to OCR logic and overall app stability.

v1.3 - The Living Interface
Quality of Life

Visual Table: The main table in the Collection tab was redesigned to include an image column for each Pokémon.

Quality of Life

General UI Improvements: Enhancements across all tabs for a smoother experience.

v1.3.8 - The "WOW" Factor
NEW

Evolution Animation System: Evolution now triggers a visual spectacle! The system was built from scratch to be modular and impactful.

NEW

Generative Sound System: Sound effects for evolution are procedurally generated by the app, eliminating the need for external audio files.

NEW

Multi-Evolution Support: Pokémon with branched evolutions (e.g., Eevee) now open a dialog box for the user to choose the desired path.

Quality of Life

Full Customization: Added options in Settings for users to choose their preferred animation style and sound effects.

v2.0.1 - The Ultimate Knowledge Hub
NEW

The Pokédex: A brand new, fully interactive Pokédex tab, designed to mimic the classic feel of the series' device. It’s not just a list; it’s a complete information center.

Detailed info on Pokémon, base stats, type matchups (offensive and defensive), EV yields, drops, and quests.

Dynamic Stat Bars: A unique visual system showing the strength of a Pokémon's base stats relative to all others in the game.

Type Explorer: A comprehensive tool to analyze the strengths and weaknesses of every type combination.

Boss Analysis & Defensive Coverage: Tools integrated into the Pokédex to check the best types against any boss.

Map Explorer & EV Spots: Navigate through maps and automatically find the best training spots.

NEW

Comparative Analysis: A powerful tool to compare Pokémon from your collection side-by-side, projecting their final stats at level 125 to determine the best "Tank," "Attacker," or "All-Rounder."

UPDATE

Remote Data Loading: The app now attempts to fetch the latest game data directly from the repository upon launch, ensuring you always have the most up-to-date info without downloading a new app version.

Quality of Life

Full Internationalization: Complete translation for PT-BR and EN-US.

v2.0.8 - The Sinnoh Era & The Interpreter V4 (Current Version)
NEW

Data Expansion: The heavyweights have arrived! Added full support (base stats, images, and evolution) for Magmortar, Rhyperior, Magnezone, Mamoswine, Weavile, and Glaceon.

OVERHAUL

Battle Logic V4.0 ("The Interpreter"): The battle simulator received a massive intelligence update. Now, the system uses Regex to read and interpret move descriptions in real-time.

This allows simulating complex effects like Burn, Poison, and residual Trap damage (Fire Spin/Whirlpool) with precision.

The AI now correctly recognizes and applies buffs and debuffs based on percentage or level.

GAME META (Synchronization)

Critical update to reflect the official game's November/December 2025 balance patches:

Boss Protection Fix: The simulation now respects the new mechanic where the Boss Defense Rank is not affected by debuffs (only base defense is reduced), making victory calculations much more realistic.

Leech Seed Nerf: Damage calculation adjusted to reflect the in-game power reduction.

Swords Dance: Buff duration adjusted to 15 seconds.

FIX

Adjustments to Learnsets, including the addition of Night Shade for Sableye at level 21.

Next Steps
The journey is just beginning! With core features now stable and robust, the focus will shift to new functionalities and community requests. Keep an eye out for future updates!
